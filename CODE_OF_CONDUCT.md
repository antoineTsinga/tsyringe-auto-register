# Code of Conduct

We are committed to providing a friendly, safe and welcoming environment for
all contributors and users, regardless of background or experience level.

By participating in this project, you agree to:

- Be respectful and considerate
- Assume good faith
- Provide constructive feedback
- Avoid personal attacks, harassment or discriminatory language

Unacceptable behavior includes (but is not limited to):

- Harassment, discrimination or hate speech
- Threats or violent language
- Trolling, insults or personal attacks
- Publishing others' private information

Project maintainers may take any action they deem appropriate, including
warning or banning contributors who violate this Code of Conduct.

If you witness or experience unacceptable behavior, please contact the
maintainer privately so that we can address the issue.

Thank you for helping to keep this community welcoming and respectful.
